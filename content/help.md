# Help

Answers to the questions buyers ask most. If yours isn't here, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and include your order number — it's the 8-character code on your confirmation page and in your confirmation email.

## Where is my order?

Items are printed after you order, so the first few days are production rather than shipping. You'll get a shipping notification with a tracking link once the parcel is on its way. Standard US delivery is about 5–8 business days after that.

If your tracking hasn't updated in several days, or it's been more than two weeks since you ordered, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your order number and we'll chase it.

## My item arrived damaged, misprinted, or defective

We'll replace it free. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} within 30 days of delivery with:

- Your order number
- A photo of the item showing the problem

A photo is all we need to send a replacement — you don't have to send the item back.

## I ordered the wrong size, or I changed my mind

Because every item is printed to order, we can't accept returns or exchanges for size changes or a change of mind. There's no stock to return it to, and no one else the item can go to. Please use the size guide on the campaign page before ordering.

This is also why your order can't be cancelled once it's submitted for production.

## Can I return my item?

Please don't ship anything back on your own. In particular, don't use the return address printed on the parcel — that's the printing facility, not us. A package arriving there without an approved claim can't be matched to your order, so you'd lose both the item and your money.

Always email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} first. We'll tell you what to do.

## Who am I actually buying from?

{{PLATFORM_NAME}} runs the store and handles your payment and support. The campaign itself belongs to the organization named on the campaign page — a school, PTA, team, or community group — and the fundraising proceeds go to them.

Your parcel ships directly from our printing partner's facility, which is why the packaging carries no branding you'll recognize. Support always comes back to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## Where does my money go?

The price covers the cost of making and shipping the item, payment processing, and a platform fee. What's left over is the fundraising profit and goes to the organization running the campaign.

## Can I change my shipping address?

Only before the item goes into production, which is usually within a few hours of ordering. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} immediately with your order number. Once it's printed and shipped we can't reroute it.

## I never received a confirmation email

Check your spam folder first. If it isn't there, your confirmation page still works — it's the link you landed on right after paying, and it shows your order number and status. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll resend it.
